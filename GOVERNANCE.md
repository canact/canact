# Governance

This is a solo-maintained repository.

| Role | Who | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Maintainer | [@SebTardif](https://github.com/SebTardif) | Merge, release, security, and project direction |

Decisions are made by the maintainer. Open a GitHub issue before a
large change. Small, tested fixes can go straight to a pull request.
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Releases are maintainer-only. Merging the release-please PR is the
human yes. That tag starts cargo-dist (GitHub Release, Homebrew,
Scoop) and the crates.io publish job.

Security reports go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public
issue. Conduct is in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Escalate a stalled or disputed change by opening an issue and waiting
for the maintainer. There is no separate committee.
